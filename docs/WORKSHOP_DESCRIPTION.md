## 简体中文

标题：`黑夜长留`

```bbcode
[h1]黑夜长留[/h1]
“黑夜必须为我而留。”

《黑夜长留》是《杀戮尖塔 2》的大型角色扩展模组，以《艾尔登法环：黑夜君临》中的角色形象与战斗特色为设计蓝本，将渡夜者们转化为拥有独立牌组与专属机制的尖塔角色。

[h2]当前内容[/h2]
现已加入三名可玩角色：
[list]
[*][b]守护者[/b]
[*][b]铁之眼[/b]
[*][b]复仇者[/b]
[/list]
每名角色均拥有完整的卡牌、遗物、药水以及专属战斗机制，可以独立完成一局游戏。

后续计划将《艾尔登法环：黑夜君临》中的所有渡夜者陆续加入本模组。欢迎各位渡夜者与爬塔玩家下载试玩！

如果你在游玩过程中发现问题，或对角色设计与平衡性有建议，欢迎加入玩家社区参与讨论。
```

## English

Title: `Night Must Stay`

```bbcode
[h1]NIGHT MUST STAY[/h1]
“The night must remain for me.”

Night Must Stay is a large character expansion mod for Slay the Spire 2. Inspired by the characters and combat styles of ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN, it reimagines the Nightfarers as Spire characters with independent card pools and unique mechanics.

[h2]Current Content[/h2]
Three playable characters are currently included:
[list]
[*][b]Guardian[/b]
[*][b]Ironeye[/b]
[*][b]Revenant[/b]
[/list]
Each character has a complete set of cards, relics, potions, and exclusive combat mechanics, and can complete a full run independently.

We plan to add every Nightfarer from ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN to the mod over time. Nightfarers and Spire climbers alike are welcome to download it and give it a try!

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
When playing multiplayer, [b]every participating player must install this mod[/b]. All players should also use the same mod version whenever possible.

[h2]Development Status[/h2]
This mod remains under active development and may contain bugs, balance issues, or unfinished designs.

Future development will continue to focus on:
[list]
[*]Balance and mechanical adjustments
[*]Bug fixes and compatibility improvements
[*]Refining existing character content
[*]Creating and implementing more Nightfarers
[/list]
If you encounter a problem or have feedback about character design or balance, you are welcome to join the community and take part in the discussion.
```
