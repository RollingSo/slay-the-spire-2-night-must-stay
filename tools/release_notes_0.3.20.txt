中文

• 修复沉睡甲虫被击晕后可能无法正常苏醒的问题。

English
• Fixed the Block-retention conflict between Sturdy Clamp and Fortify: when both apply, the higher retention cap is used. Barricade still preserves all Block.
• Rebalanced Guardian cards. Defend Reinforcement now grants 9/13 Fortify and Exhausts. Fearless now grants 6 temporary Strength and loses 2 temporary Dexterity. Phantom Slash now also applies Imbalance on its first attack. Adjusted several other card values and rarities.
• Fixed Wings of Salvation's damage reduction and enemy damage-intent refresh.
• Fixed Soul Departure's multiplayer selection handoff. Damage to Revenant is now absorbed in order by Necros, Osty, and family summons, with remaining damage passed to Revenant.
• Fixed Slumbering Beetles potentially failing to wake up after being stunned.
• Optimized Duchess's time reversal and Midnight Waltz while preserving their visuals and timing.
• Adjusted Revenant's displayed size at shops and rest sites.
