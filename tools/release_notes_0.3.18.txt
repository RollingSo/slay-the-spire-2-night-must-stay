• Adjusted Imbalance's stun thresholds: initially 2 stacks in solo play, or 3/5/7 stacks with 2/3/4 players. Each stun increases that enemy's next threshold by 1, up to 3 additional stacks. This resets in a new combat. Also corrected its hover-tip description.
• Corrected Stalwart Shield's status description to match its effect: at turn start, add a card with Defend or Defense in its name from the draw pile to your hand.
• Increased Weeping Strings' Energy cost from 1 to 2.
• Fixed Soul Departure's multiplayer group-selection flow to prevent teammates' selection screens failing to open or the selection process getting stuck.
• Unified health-bar widths for Revenant's family summons and Necros, and corrected health-bar and Block-icon alignment under scaling.
• Updated Origin's card artwork.
