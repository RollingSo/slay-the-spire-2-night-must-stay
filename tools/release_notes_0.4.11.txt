[h1]0.4.11｜卡牌平衡与卡图更新[/h1]

- 圣光羽阵：下回合少抽的牌从 2 张减至 1 张。
- 焰光照雪：每回合首次获得活力时，对随机敌人施加的冻伤从 1 层提高到 2 层。
- 向死而生：耗能从 0 提高到 1。现在每回合开始时少抽 1 张牌，并将 2 张随机绝境牌加入手牌。
- 更新铁之眼「共享情报」和复仇者「为我而战」的卡图。
- 修复部分卡牌在详情界面的大图显示，并精简发布包中的未使用资源。

[h1]0.4.11 | Card Balance and Artwork Updates[/h1]

- Sacred Feather Formation: The next-turn draw penalty has been reduced from 2 cards to 1.
- Flamelight on Snow: The first time you gain Vigor each turn, apply 2 Freeze to a random enemy, up from 1.
- Life from Death: Energy cost increased from 0 to 1. At the start of each turn, draw 1 fewer card and add 2 random Desperation cards to your hand.
- Updated the artwork for Ironeye's Shared Intelligence and Revenant's Fight for Me.
- Fixed the large portraits for some cards in the card detail screen and removed unused assets from the release package.
