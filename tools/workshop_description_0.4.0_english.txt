[h1]黑夜长留 | Night Must Stay[/h1]
[b]Current version: 0.4.0 | Supports the official and Public Beta branches of Slay the Spire 2[/b]

The Nightfarers of ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN have entered the Spire!

This mod aims to bring each Nightfarer's distinctive fighting style into deckbuilding gameplay, with a design approach and balance close to the original Slay the Spire 2.

Five playable characters are currently available: Guardian, Ironeye, Revenant, Duchess, and Wylder. Each has their own cards, relics, and unique combat mechanics.

[b]New in 0.4.0: Wylder[/b]
Use the claw to enter Pursuit and unleash heavy attacks that break the enemy's stance. Wylder arrives with a complete 90-card pool, 9 dedicated relics, and 3 dedicated potions, supporting archetypes built around Pursuit, Break, Desperation, Vigor, Freeze, and Imbalance.

Nightfarers, let the night linger within the Spire.

[h1]Mod Information[/h1]
[b]Supported languages:[/b] Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean
[b]Required mods:[/b] None
[b]Multiplayer:[/b] All players must install this mod and use the same version
